Ask your phone to find a used console, compare the listings and get the purchase ready, then just hit confirm. That is the promise of Muse, the personal AI agent Meta launched in the United States on September 8. In less than two weeks, it overtook ChatGPT on the US App Store, got blocked by Amazon and was welcomed by Shopify.
