France, Germany, Spain and Italy generated €498,779,919 on TikTok Shop in the ninety days to 9 July 2026, a window that covers most of the second quarter. That figure comes from Kalodata's estimated data for the four markets.* The number itself is the least interesting thing about it. What matters is where it came from.
