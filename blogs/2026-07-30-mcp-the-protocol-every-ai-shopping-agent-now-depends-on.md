In November 2024, Anthropic published a technical specification with a name only an engineer could love: the Model Context Protocol (MCP) . There was no keynote, no retail partner on stage, no promise to reinvent shopping. Twenty months later, it sits underneath virtually every AI commerce initiative on the market.
