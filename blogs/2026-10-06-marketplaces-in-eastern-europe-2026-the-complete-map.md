If you're planning an e-commerce push into Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovenia, Croatia, Serbia, Greece or the three Baltic states, the playbook you used for France, Germany or Iberia is not the right one. Western Europe organises itself around one or two global giants per country. This part of the continent grew its own.
